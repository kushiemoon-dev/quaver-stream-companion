QuaverStreamCompanion

Your Quaver plays, live on stream.

A small app that follows your Quaver games from the files the game already writes, and feeds eight ready-made OBS overlays from your own machine: the map you play, your result, your session, your keys.

Download v0.2.0 Try the overlays

Where it stands

  • Windows 10 and later tested, latest release v0.2.0
  • Linux tested in CI with simulated Steam folders, not yet with a real Quaver install
  • macOS not supported yet
  • Network none: the server listens on 127.0.0.1 only

The real overlays, running here

These are the overlay pages that ship with qsc, in frames. Instead of your game, a simulator on this page sends them made-up plays with the same events qsc sends: watch the rank chip, the Best badge and the session numbers change. The demo runs the overlays from the current source, which can be slightly ahead of v0.2.0.

Press Play a map, or scroll here and one play starts by itself.

The curve and history overlays read your saved plays from qsc, which this page cannot do, so they show their built-in demo data. In OBS each overlay is a Browser source; sizes and options are in the overlay guide, and the theme guide shows how to make your own look.

Up and running in three steps

  1. Download

    Get the archive for your system from the releases page, check it against checksums.txt and unzip it. There is no installer.

  2. Run it

    Start qsc --open. It finds your Quaver folder, serves everything on your own machine and opens the dashboard in your browser.

  3. Add the overlays in OBS

    On the Overlays page, copy a link and paste it into an OBS Browser source. Then play a map: the overlay appears when you start and hides in the menu.

Files only, and nothing leaves your machine

  • It only reads files Quaver already writes: Logs/runtime.log and quaver.db, which is opened read-only.
  • It never reads game memory, never modifies or injects into the game, and never writes to the Quaver folder.
  • No account, no telemetry and no online service. The app makes no network request, and its server listens on 127.0.0.1 only.
  • Lane keys and KPS are opt-in, off by default, and work on Windows only.
